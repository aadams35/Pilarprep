# Hackathon Demo Script

## 90-Second Flow

1. Pick `Apex Mutual`.
2. Show that PillarPrep captures industry, meeting type, company size, ranked pillar priorities, known context, and approved decision-maker notes.
3. Click `Generate / refine brief`.
4. Open the generated technical brief and point out AWS Well-Architected alignment.
5. Switch to the executive brief and point out the low-jargon business framing.
6. Open the stakeholder lens and show how manually provided profile notes become tailored questions and validation points.
7. Apply feedback such as `Reduce AWS jargon` or `Focus on security`.
8. Approve the pre-brief.
9. Promote the brief into Project Brain.
10. Ask as `PM`: `Create the first two-week plan.`
11. Open Project Brain and point out the generated sprint plan, risk list, stakeholder map, and follow-up email artifacts.
12. Close with the AWS story: Bedrock generates and refines, S3 stores artifacts, DynamoDB tracks state, Knowledge Bases ground follow-up, and Strands can orchestrate Project Brain tools.

## One-Sentence Pitch

PillarPrep turns SA meeting prep into a two-phase AWS-native workflow: first a refined customer and stakeholder pre-brief, then a living Project Brain for everyone responsible for follow-through.
