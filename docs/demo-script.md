# Hackathon Demo Script

## 90-Second Flow

1. Pick `Apex Mutual`.
2. Show that PillarPrep captures industry, meeting type, company size, pillar priorities, and known context.
3. Click `Generate / refine brief`.
4. Open the generated technical brief and point out AWS Well-Architected alignment.
5. Switch to the executive brief and point out the low-jargon business framing.
6. Apply feedback such as `Reduce AWS jargon` or `Focus on security`.
7. Approve the pre-brief.
8. Promote the brief into Project Brain.
9. Ask as `PM`: `Create the first two-week plan.`
10. Close with the AWS story: Bedrock generates and refines, S3 stores artifacts, DynamoDB tracks state, Knowledge Bases ground follow-up, and Strands can orchestrate Project Brain tools.

## One-Sentence Pitch

PillarPrep turns SA meeting prep into a two-phase AWS-native workflow: first a refined customer pre-brief, then a living Project Brain for everyone responsible for follow-through.
